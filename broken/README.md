# Brightdesk signup flow (current version)

Static page: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`. Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Step 1 - Tell us about you
Fields: full name, email, repeat email, password, phone number, birthdate (DD/MM/YYYY), company, job title, street address, country, how did you hear about us. Checkboxes: weekly news and offers, accept the terms (terms open in a dialog).
Button: Next.

## Step 2 - Pick one
Plans: Free, Pro, Team. Select one.
Button: Continue.

## Step 3 - Payment
Fields: card number, expiry (MM/YY), CVC.
Button: Proceed.

## Then
- Check your inbox: a message is "sent" to the email entered. Button: Submit.
- Confirmation: welcome message with the chosen plan.
